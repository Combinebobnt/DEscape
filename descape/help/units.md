# Units

Units mode places, selects, moves and edits objects: units, buildings, trees, resources and scenery. The left panel holds the object catalog and Owner, with the Inspector below. Each tool's options appear on the Tool Options row under the toolbar.

Where this is in the in-game editor: the Units tab. Convert matches its Converting, and the Eraser the Terrain tab's Erase.

## Picking an object

The Units, Buildings, Heroes and Other buttons show one category of the catalog. The class dropdown narrows it, the filter box finds an object by name or Unit ID, and Icons, Names and Grid change the view. Tick "Show editor-hidden objects" for objects the game's editor hides. "Villager (Random)" places a male or female villager at random. Double-click an object, or press Enter on it, to switch to Place Unit.

Owner sets who owns what you place. Objects the game's editor lists only for GAIA (wild animals, resources, trees, rocks, relics and more) are hidden while Owner is a player, and always go to GAIA; **Settings > Advanced** can keep them listed. Clicking an object, or moving to it with the arrow keys, shows its portrait, Unit ID and base stats in the Inspector, even with units selected. The selection stays selected, and Delete and the Edit menu still act on it; its fields show again when you select, place or edit units, or click Show selection.

Portraits follow the owner, as Age of Empires II shows them in play: a player whose architecture (or, with no architecture set, civilization) has its own portrait for an object shows that one, in the catalog for the Owner and in the Inspector for the selected unit's owner. GAIA, and a selection with more than one owner, show the standard portrait.

## Placing objects

**Place Unit** puts the picked object down one click at a time, for the chosen owner, snapped to the grid and centred on the cursor. A preview follows the cursor. Each click is one undo step, and the new unit becomes the selection; after a place into a building or unit, that host is selected instead.

- The game's placement rules apply: the object needs terrain it can stand on and room clear of other objects. A refused click places nothing and the status log under the map says why. Tick Free placement to place it anyway.
- The preview shows a refusal before you click: where the game would refuse the object, a sprite preview is tinted red, and a coloured mark (in Flat, or for an object with no sprite) is filled red and cross-hatched. It updates without a mouse move when you tick Free placement or place a unit under it.
- Point at a building or unit that can hold the object, such as a castle, a transport ship or a ram, and the click puts it inside, at the host's spot. The host is outlined and the preview is tinted yellow. Where the host is full or can't take that object, the preview is red and the click places nothing. With Free placement ticked, the object is placed on the map as before.
- With **View > Layers > Fish Outlines** on, a picked fish's preview is ringed like a placed one, here and in the Unit Brush.
- Tick Off-grid, or hold Alt for one click, to place exactly under the cursor.
- To turn the object before you click, hover the map and use the Rotate commands' keys or the mouse wheel's rotate binding ([Keyboard and Mouse](keyboard-and-mouse.md) shows both). Units turn, trees and scenery step through their variants, gates switch orientation, and later clicks keep that facing or variant.

**Unit Brush** paints the picked object across the brush in one drag, as one undo step. Density is the chance each tile gets one; every tile is rolled once per drag, so going over it again adds nothing, and a drag never stacks objects on itself. The same placement rules apply, and Free placement lifts them. While you hover, a see-through copy of the object shows on the tile under the cursor, red if that tile would refuse it. It shows one object, not the Density or the random facings, and the gold outline shows the area. The Rotate commands and the wheel's Rotate preview binding (or its "or selection" form) turn that copy, and the next drags paint the facing or variant it shows; the wheel's Brush size or rotate preview binding (or its "or selection" form) resizes the brush here instead. With Random facing/variant ticked, each object gets a random facing, or a random variant for trees and scenery; unticked, each gets the one last set by turning the preview, here or in Place Unit, or by the Eyedropper. Turning the preview unticks Random facing/variant, and ticking it again goes back to random. Off-grid, or Alt held as the drag starts, sets each object a little off its tile's centre. The Unit Brush refuses walls, gates and cliffs: walls are drawn with Place Unit, Unit Line or Unit Shape, and gates and cliffs have their own tools.

**Unit Line** and **Unit Shape** draw the picked object in a line, or in the Shape chosen on the Tool Options row inside the box you drag: Rectangle, Ellipse, Triangle (its base along the box edge where the drag started, its point at the middle of the far edge) or Right Triangle (its right angle where the drag started). Tick Filled to fill the shape, not just its outline. Each drag is one undo step. The objects sit on a grid of cells as big as the largest picked object, and Spacing leaves that many empty tiles between cells, so a rectangle of houses at Spacing 1 leaves a lane between each pair. Hold Shift for a line at an even angle, a square, a circle, or a triangle with 45-degree sides. Without Shift, a flat triangle's sides leave the base a few tiles in from its corners. The placement rules, Free placement and Random facing/variant work as for the Unit Brush. The pick and the options are read when the drag starts, and a gate, a cliff, or a wall picked with anything else (another wall included) starts no drag; the status log says why. With a wall picked, they lay a joined wall along the line or around the shape's outline, ignoring Filled and Spacing, as below.

### Walls and gates

With a wall picked, Place Unit and Unit Line drag a run that bends once; Shift makes it straight, and right-click or Esc cancels. Unit Shape lays walls around the outline of its Shape; Shift makes a square, a circle or an even triangle, whose sharp corners get a small cluster of towers. The Wall button on the Tool Options row picks the last wall you used, and its arrow lists every wall.

New walls join and reshape the walls they touch, always snap to tiles, and ignore Off-grid and Free placement. "Walls skip occupied tiles" leaves a gap at trees, resources, buildings, units, obstacles and invisible blockers (passable eye candy never blocks), and a wall or gate is never doubled. A gate placed on a wall line replaces the walls under it and reshapes those beside it. A moved wall takes the shape of its new place, and the walls beside where it left and where it landed reshape too; a moved wall left standing alone becomes a tower. Deleting or cutting a wall or gate reshapes the walls left beside it, so a run's new end becomes an end piece. Pasted walls and gates do the same: each pasted wall takes the shape of where it lands, and the walls it lands beside reshape.

## Selecting and moving

With **Select**, click a unit to select it; click the same spot again to walk down a stack. Ctrl+click toggles a unit, Shift+click adds one, and a plain click on empty ground clears the selection. Drag to draw a box, even starting on a unit: Ctrl+drag toggles what it covers and Shift+drag adds it. **Edit > Select All** selects every unit the filters show, **Edit > Deselect** or Esc clears the selection, and **Edit > Select Whole Stack** adds every unit stacked with a selected one. The selection stays when you switch to Terrain mode.

With **Move**, drag a unit to move it, or a selected unit to move the whole selection. On a stack, the drag takes its selected unit, so pick a lower one with Select first. Placement rules, Free placement, Off-grid and Alt work as for Place Unit, and Esc cancels the drag. The dragged preview turns red where the drop would be refused; in a group drag only the units that can't go there turn red, though the whole group stays put on release. A drop onto a building that can garrison the units never shows red, and one that is full or can't hold them always does. Drop units on a building that can hold them to garrison them; Shift keeps it a plain move. Whatever a unit garrisons moves with it. With "Moving a gate updates walls" ticked, a moved gate clears the walls at its new place and fills the gap it leaves with wall. A gate's own corner pieces never block its move.

To turn a unit as you move it, use the Rotate commands of **Edit > Rotate Selection** or the mouse wheel's rotate binding during the drag ([Keyboard and Mouse](keyboard-and-mouse.md) shows both). The preview turns and the unit itself stays as it is until you let go, so Esc or a refused drop leaves it untouched, and the move and the turn are one undo step. Units turn, trees and scenery step through their variants, and a gate switches orientation and lands by its new shape, with walls updating for that shape when "Moving a gate updates walls" is ticked (unticked, it can land on walls, as any moved gate can). In a group drag each unit turns on its own spot, and a gate that would hang off the map there keeps its orientation. Drop a turned unit back where it was and only the turn is applied. A drop into a building garrisons the unit without the turn.

Under any tool, with the map focused, the arrow keys nudge the selection by a tenth of a tile, or a whole tile with Shift, and Delete removes it without asking (**Edit > Undo** brings it back). Deleting a building also deletes what it garrisons. A nudge and a paste skip the placement rules.

## Turning, variants and copies

**Edit > Rotate Selection** and the two Rotate buttons on the toolbar turn each selected unit about its own centre. Gates cycle through their four orientations, and each turn clears the walls under the gate's new shape and reshapes those beside it, as placing a gate does; the Rotation field does the same. Trees and scenery step through their graphic variants, and walls, cliffs and single-frame objects are skipped. While Place Unit's or the Unit Brush's preview is on the map, the Rotate keys turn the preview instead (not during a Unit Brush drag, which keeps the facing it started with), and during a Move drag they turn the drag. The mouse wheel turns the selected units the same way when one of its rows is set to one of its "or selection" choices in [Settings](settings.md) > Keybinds, except while Place Unit or the Unit Brush has an object to place, when the wheel turns only its preview. **Edit > Cycle Variant** picks the previous, next or a random variant for trees, plants, rocks and scenery.

To flip or turn several units as one group, select them and use **Edit > Flip Selection > Flip West ↔ North** or **Flip West ↔ South**, or **Edit > Rotate Selection > Rotate Group ↻ (Quarter Turn)** or **Rotate Group ↺ (Quarter Turn)**. The group mirrors across the edges of the box around the selected units, swapping the two corners the entry names, or turns about its centre, and whatever they garrison goes along. Each unit stays the same unit with the same owner, so triggers that name it still find it. Facings turn with the group, a gate takes the orientation that matches its new direction, and a wall takes the shape of its new run; cliffs and objects with no turned shape keep theirs, and the status log under the map counts them. Flip and turn as often as you like: the whole run is one undo step. Press Enter to keep the result, or Esc to put everything back as it was before the first flip; a click elsewhere or any other edit keeps it too. If a unit would land on something in the way, nothing changes and the status log says why; tick Free placement to allow it. Nothing may land off the map.

With units selected, **Edit > Copy Units** and **Edit > Cut Units** take them and whatever they garrison. **Edit > Paste Units** centres them on the tile under the cursor as one undo step, and selects them. Copies are kept in **Edit > Clipboard History…**; for regions of terrain, see [Terrain](terrain.md).

## The Inspector

For one selected unit the Inspector shows its portrait, name, Unit ID and stats, then Owner, X, Y, Z, Rotation, Capture Flag, Unit State, Reference ID and Garrisoned in. Each change is one undo step. After you click an object in the catalog, or pick one with the Eyedropper or the Wall button, the Inspector shows that object instead, with how many units are selected and a Show selection button that brings their fields back.

Rotation is a facing number, a variant number for trees and scenery, or a gate's orientation, 0 to 3. Walls and cliffs are read-only, because the game works out their shape; a wall standing alone or at the end of a run is drawn as a tower, as in the game, whatever number the file stores. Click or scroll the dial beside it to turn the unit. Dragging the dial turns a unit as you go, and the whole drag is one undo step; a gate turns when you let go. Capture Flag needs a scenario saved by the September 2026 game update (version 1.59).

With several units selected, a field shows their shared value or "(mixed)". A typed value applies to all of them, and the X, Y and Z arrows move every one by a step, keeping the formation. Rotation's arrows and wheel turn every one by a step: over "(mixed)" each unit keeps its own facing, and only a typed value or a click on the dial lines them up. In a group of units with different direction counts, Rotation runs on the finest count, so a unit with fewer directions turns on every other step or so.

## Garrisons

A unit that can hold others shows a Garrison list. Its units are hidden on the map unless **Filters > Show Garrisoned Units** is on; then double-click a row to select one. Add... creates an object inside, and Pick from map puts the units you click inside until Esc or a right-click. Duplicate adds copies of the selected rows inside the same host, each with its row's owner. Cut, Copy and Paste move or copy rows between hosts; Cut keeps their IDs, so triggers that name them still work. Unload puts rows on the tile beside the host, Delete removes them, and Owner... gives them a new owner.

## Other tools

The Tool Options row's settings here (Free placement, Off-grid, the wall the Wall button picks, the Unit Brush's Density and Random facing/variant, Unit Line and Unit Shape's Shape, Filled, Spacing and Random facing/variant, Convert's Sources and the Eraser's Targets, among others) keep what you set until you close DEscape. **Settings > Tools** sets what they start at.

- **Convert**: drag over the units of the players ticked under Sources to give them to the Owner. GAIA-only objects stay with GAIA.
- **Eraser** works as in Terrain mode; see [Terrain](terrain.md).
- **Eyedropper**: click a unit to make its type, owner and facing or variant the next placement. Shift+click takes the type only. With **Switch back after a pick** ticked, a pick returns to the tool you used before the Eyedropper; Ctrl+click picks and stays, so repeat Ctrl+clicks walk down a stack.
- **Pan** and **Ruler** work in every mode; [Keyboard and Mouse](keyboard-and-mouse.md) lists every gesture.
